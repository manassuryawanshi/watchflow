Focus Mode

Decide what you're here to learn.

Focus Mode turns YouTube into a true learning-only environment. When enabled, your queue becomes a strict educational allowlist. Unapproved videos and algorithmic pathways are intercepted with a calming intentional block screen.

  • ✓ Strict Allowlist: Only queued videos and course lessons can be viewed.
  • ✓ No Algorithmic Drift: Autoplay, sidebars, and Up Next cannot bypass your learning list.
  • ✓ Shorts Redirection: Direct Shorts links open in standard player with scrubber controls.
  • ✓ Fast Keyboard Shortcut: Press Alt + F to toggle Focus Mode instantly.

Supporting Architecture

Crafted for serious focus.

Small details that make studying on YouTube frictionless and sustainable.

Window Fullscreen W

Expands the YouTube video to fill 100% of your browser window without entering OS fullscreen. Tabs and address bar stay accessible.

Local Learning Analytics

Track your actual study time versus passive browsing across Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, and 30 Days. Stored 100% locally on your machine.

Configurable Daily Limits

Choose between gentle notification toasts (Soft Mode) or an enforceable calming Cooldown break screen (Hard Mode) when study time ends.

Scheduled Study Windows

Automate Focus Mode enforcement during work hours or study periods (e.g. 09:00 to 13:00) so focus requires zero willpower.

Course Mode Banner

When studying inside a queued course, an in-video banner shows lesson numbers, progress percentages, and direct next-lesson navigation.

JSON Export & Wipe Controls

Full sovereign control over your data. Export your entire queue and analytics to a JSON backup or wipe local storage in one click.

Privacy Architecture

Your learning data stays yours.

WatchFlow operates on a strict local-first philosophy. We do not track your activity, collect watch history, or transmit analytics to external servers.

100% Local Storage

Your queue, lesson checkmarks, watch stats, and settings are saved directly in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

Zero Remote Servers

No backend servers, API proxies, cloud databases, or tracking endpoints. Core features require zero internet communication.

No YouTube API Key

WatchFlow functions entirely client-side without requiring you to create Google Cloud accounts or paste YouTube Data API keys.
