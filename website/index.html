The Friction

YouTube was built for engagement.
Your attention deserves intention.

You open YouTube to watch a single lecture or tutorial. Within minutes, infinite recommendation algorithms, vertical Shorts carousels, and Up Next autoplay remove every natural stopping point.

— Infinite home recommendations
— Distracting Shorts reels
— Autoplay drift
— No natural stopping point
Learning-Only Mode

Turn YouTube into a place to learn.

Focus Mode removes the paths that pull you away from what you actually intended to watch.

  • Strict Educational Allowlist: Only content intentionally saved to your Learning Queue can be played.
  • Off-Topic Videos Intercepted: Unapproved videos and algorithmic suggestions trigger a calming intentional block screen.
  • Discovery Paths Suppressed: Feeds, sidebars, and Up Next cannot bypass your learning boundary.
  • Shorts Redirection: Direct Shorts links automatically redirect to the standard player with scrub controls.
Structured Courses

Turn videos into a curriculum.

Save videos and playlists to your Learning Queue and work through them with progress tracking.

Focus Home

Always know what to watch next.

YouTube Home becomes your daily productivity command center. Instead of asking "What is YouTube suggesting right now?", WatchFlow answers: "What should I do right now?"

  • Today's Progress: Live daily study minute tracking against your chosen target.
  • Continue Learning: One-click action resuming the next incomplete lesson in your active course.
  • Current Curriculum: Single-line Next-Up preview with overflow protection and full title tooltips.
  • Recent Learning: Seamless access to your latest study sessions without endless distraction.
Automated Boundaries

Boundaries that work automatically.

Decide your boundaries once. Let WatchFlow enforce them.

Daily Limits & Cooldown

Configure daily study caps with gentle warning toasts (Soft Mode) or an enforced, calming Cooldown break screen (Hard Mode) when your limit is reached.

Scheduled Study Windows

Automate Focus Mode activation during dedicated study or rest hours (e.g. 09:00–13:00). Focus becomes an automatic default rather than a willpower test.
